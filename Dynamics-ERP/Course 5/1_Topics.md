# 💰 Order-to-Cash & Cash Management Fundamentals (D365FO)

---

## 🔍 Overview

This document explains how:
- Sales generate revenue
- Cash is collected
- ERP tracks both financial and operational impact

---

# 🛒 Order-to-Cash (O2C)

## 📌 Definition

Order-to-Cash is:
> The process from selling goods to collecting payment

---

## 🔁 O2C Lifecycle

1. Create sales order  
2. Deliver goods (optional at this level)  
3. Create invoice  
4. Receive payment  
5. Settle invoice  

---

# 🧾 Key Concept – Revenue vs Cash

> Revenue is recorded when goods/services are invoiced  
> Cash is recorded when payment is received  

---

## ⚠️ Important Insight

A company can:
- Be profitable ✅  
- But have no cash ❌  

---

# 📊 Financial Impact

## Example – Sales Transaction

### Step 1 – Invoice

- Revenue ↑ (P&L)
- Customer receivable ↑ (Asset)

---

### Step 2 – Payment

- Cash ↑ (Asset)
- Customer receivable ↓

---

# 🏦 Cash & Bank Management

## 📌 What is it?

Cash and Bank Management tracks:
- Bank accounts
- Incoming payments
- Outgoing payments

---

## ✅ Key Functions

- Manage bank balances
- Record customer receipts
- Record vendor payments
- Reconcile transactions

---

# 🔄 Settlement Concept

### 📌 What is Settlement?

Settlement is:
> Matching a payment to an invoice

---

## ✅ Example

| Invoice | Payment | Status |
|--------|--------|--------|
| €1,000 | €1,000 | Settled ✅ |

---

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Confusing revenue with cash
- Forgetting to settle transactions
- Not tracking receivables properly
- Poor customer data management

---

# ✅ Key Takeaways

- O2C drives revenue
- Cash management tracks liquidity
- Profit and cash are not the same
- ERP tracks both flows separately
