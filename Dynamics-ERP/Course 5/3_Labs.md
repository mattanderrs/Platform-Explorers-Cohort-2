# Lab 05 – Order-to-Cash & Cash Management

## 🎯 Objective

Execute the full sales and cash cycle in D365FO.

---

## 📍 Scenario

BlueContoso sells products to customers and receives payments.

---

## ✅ Prerequisites

- Week 04 completed (inventory + P2P)
- Products available for sale
- GL setup complete

---

## 🔧 Task 1 – Create Customer

1. Create customer record
2. Assign:
   - Payment terms (30 days)
   - Currency

---

## 🛒 Task 2 – Create Sales Order

1. Add product
2. Enter quantity and price
3. Confirm order

---

## 🧾 Task 3 – Invoice Customer

1. Post sales invoice
2. Verify:
   - Revenue recorded
   - Customer balance created

---

## 💰 Task 4 – Receive Payment

1. Record customer payment
2. Select bank account
3. Apply payment to invoice

---

## 🔄 Task 5 – Settlement

1. Match payment with invoice
2. Confirm settlement

---

## ✅ Validation Checklist

- Customer created
- Invoice posted
- Payment recorded
- Invoice settled

---

## 🤔 Reflection Questions

- What happens if payment is not received?
- What happens if partial payment is made?
- Why is settlement important?
