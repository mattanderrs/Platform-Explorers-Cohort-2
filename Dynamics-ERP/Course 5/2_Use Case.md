# Use Case – BlueContoso Trading Ltd
## Order-to-Cash & Cash Cycle

---

## 📍 Business Situation

BlueContoso is now fully operational:

- Goods are purchased (Week 4)
- Inventory is available

Now the company needs to:
✅ Sell goods  
✅ Invoice customers  
✅ Collect payments  

---

## 🛒 Scenario

BlueContoso sells laptops to a customer with **30-day payment terms**.

---

## 🧾 Requirements

- Track customer balances
- Record revenue
- Manage delayed payments
- Ensure accurate cash tracking

---

## 🔁 Business Process

1. Create customer  
2. Create sales order  
3. Generate invoice  
4. Receive payment  
5. Settle invoice  

---

## ❓ Key Questions

1. When is revenue recognized?
2. When is cash received?
3. What happens if the customer delays payment?
4. How does ERP track outstanding balances?

---

## ✅ Expected Outcome

- Revenue correctly recorded  
- Customer receivable tracked  
- Cash inflow recorded  
- Transactions settled  

---

## 🧠 Consultant Thinking

- Always evaluate:
  - Payment terms
  - Customer credit risk
- Ensure:
  - Proper settlement process
  - Accurate reporting of outstanding balances
