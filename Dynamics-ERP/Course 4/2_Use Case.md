# Use Case – BlueContoso Trading Ltd
## Procure-to-Pay Execution

---

## 📍 Business Situation

BlueContoso has:
- Vendors (Week 3)
- Products (Week 3)
- Financial structure (Week 2)

The company is now ready to:
✅ Purchase goods  
✅ Receive inventory  
✅ Record vendor invoices  

---

## 📦 Scenario

BlueContoso orders laptops from a supplier.

---

## 🧾 Requirements

- Track inventory properly
- Record financial liability
- Ensure accurate cost tracking

---

## 🔁 Business Process

1. Create purchase order  
2. Receive goods into inventory  
3. Post vendor invoice  
4. Update financial accounts  

---

## ❓ Key Questions

1. When does inventory increase?
2. When is the vendor liability recorded?
3. What accounts are affected?
4. What happens if invoice differs from receipt?

---

## ✅ Expected Outcome

- Accurate inventory records  
- Correct financial posting  
- Traceable procurement process  

---

## 🧠 Consultant Thinking

- Always validate:
  - Quantity vs invoice
  - Price vs agreement
- Understand financial timing differences
