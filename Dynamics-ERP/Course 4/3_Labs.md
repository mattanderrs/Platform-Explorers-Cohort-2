# Lab 04 – Inventory & Procure-to-Pay

## 🎯 Objective

Execute a full procurement cycle in D365FO and observe:
- Inventory movement
- Financial impact

---

## 📍 Scenario

BlueContoso purchases laptops from a vendor and stores them in inventory.

---

## ✅ Prerequisites

- Week 03 completed (vendors + products)
- Week 02 completed (GL structure)

---

## 🔧 Task 1 – Create Purchase Order

1. Select vendor
2. Add product (Laptop)
3. Enter quantity and price
4. Confirm order

---

## 📦 Task 2 – Product Receipt

1. Receive goods
2. Post product receipt
3. Check:
   - Inventory quantity increased

---

## 🧾 Task 3 – Vendor Invoice

1. Create invoice from PO
2. Post invoice
3. Verify:
   - Vendor payable recorded

---

## 📊 Task 4 – Review Financial Impact

Check:

- Inventory account
- Vendor payable account

---

## ✅ Validation Checklist

- Purchase order created
- Inventory updated
- Invoice posted
- Financial entries generated

---

## 🤔 Reflection Questions

- What happens if goods are received but not invoiced?
- What happens if invoice price differs from PO?
- Why is timing important?
