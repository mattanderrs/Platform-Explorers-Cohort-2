# 📦 Inventory & Procure-to-Pay Fundamentals (D365FO)

## 🔍 Overview

This document explains how purchasing, inventory, and finance are connected in ERP systems.

---

# 📦 Inventory Basics

## 📌 What is Inventory?

Inventory represents:
> Physical goods owned by the company for sale or use

---

## 🧠 Key Concept

> Inventory is an **asset** (Balance Sheet)

---

## ✅ Examples

- Laptops
- Office equipment
- Raw materials

---

# ⚙️ Inventory Behavior in ERP

Inventory involves:

- Quantity tracking
- Value tracking
- Movement tracking

---

## 📊 Inventory Impact

| Action | Impact |
|------|-------|
| Purchase | Inventory increases |
| Sale | Inventory decreases |

---

# 🛒 Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Process

## 📌 Definition

Procure-to-Pay covers:
> From ordering goods to paying the supplier

---

## 🔁 P2P Lifecycle

1. Create Purchase Order  
2. Receive goods (Product Receipt)  
3. Record vendor invoice  
4. Pay vendor  

---

# 🔄 Key Concept – Timing Difference

### Product Receipt
- Inventory increases
- No financial liability yet (in some configurations)

### Vendor Invoice
- Liability is recorded
- Financial posting occurs

---

# 📊 Financial Impact

## Example: Buying Goods

### Step 1 – Product Receipt
- Inventory (Asset) ↑

### Step 2 – Vendor Invoice
- Payables (Liability) ↑

---

# 🧠 ERP Integration Insight

> Inventory and Finance are tightly integrated

Every purchase impacts:
- Stock levels
- Financial accounting

---

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Confusing receipt with invoice
- Not understanding inventory valuation
- Ignoring financial impact of transactions

---

# ✅ Key Takeaways

- Inventory is a Balance Sheet asset
- P2P drives both operations and finance
- Timing matters (receipt vs invoice)
- ERP ensures traceability and control
