# Lab 06 – Mini ERP Implementation (Gricae Enterprises)

## 🎯 Objective

Simulate a small ERP implementation by applying knowledge from Weeks 1–5.

---

## 📍 Scenario

You are part of an implementation team deploying D365FO for Gricae Enterprises.

---

## ✅ Tasks

### Step 1 – Requirement Mapping

Identify:

- Finance needs
- Procurement needs
- Sales needs

---

### Step 2 – Define Setup Scope

List:

- Legal entity setup
- Financial setup
- Master data required

---

### Step 3 – Data Migration Plan

Define:

- What data will be migrated
- Who owns the data
- Validation process

---

### Step 4 – Workflow Design

Define:

- Approval rules for purchase orders
- Responsible roles

---

### Step 5 – Solution Walkthrough

Explain how:

- Procurement works
- Sales works
- Finance tracks everything

---

## ✅ Deliverables

- Requirement summary
- Setup checklist
- Data migration list
- Workflow design

---

## 🤔 Reflection Questions

- What is the hardest part of ERP implementation?
- What risks exist before go-live?
- What happens if data is incorrect?
