# 🏗️ ERP Implementation Fundamentals (D365FO)

---

## 🔍 Overview

This document explains how ERP systems like D365FO are implemented in real projects.

---

# 🧭 ERP Implementation Lifecycle

## 📌 Typical Phases

1. Analysis (Requirement Gathering)
2. Design (Functional Design)
3. Build (Configuration)
4. Test (Validation)
5. Deploy (Go-live)
6. Support

---

# 📋 Requirement Gathering

## 📌 What is it?

Understanding:
> How the business operates and what it needs

---

## ✅ Key Activities

- Conduct workshops
- Ask business users questions
- Document processes

---

## ✅ Example Questions

- How do you purchase goods?
- How do you manage inventory?
- How do you record financials?

---

# 📄 Documentation

## 📌 Key Documents

- Functional Requirements Document (FRD)
- Business process maps
- Data migration templates

---

# ⚙️ Configuration

## 📌 What is configured?

- Legal entities
- Chart of Accounts
- Vendors, customers, products
- Posting profiles
- Workflows

---

# 📥 Data Migration

## 📌 What is migrated?

### Master Data
- Vendors
- Customers
- Products
- Chart of Accounts

---

### Opening Balances
- GL balances
- Vendor balances
- Customer balances
- Inventory stock

---

## ⚠️ Key Challenge

> Poor data = poor ERP results

---

# ✅ Workflow & Controls

## 📌 Why workflows?

- Approval processes
- Control spending
- Ensure compliance

---

## ✅ Example

- Purchase orders > €10,000 require approval

---

# 🧠 Consultant Role

A functional consultant must:

- Translate business needs into system setup
- Guide clients through decisions
- Ensure system aligns with real operations

---

# ✅ Key Takeaways

- ERP implementation is structured and phased
- Requirements drive configuration
- Data quality is critical
- Consultants bridge business and system
