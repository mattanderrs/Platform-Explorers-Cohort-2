# Use Case – Gricae Enterprises
## D365FO Implementation Project

---

## 🏢 Company Overview

- Name: Gricae Enterprises
- Industry: Import & wholesale distribution
- Location: Malta
- Employees: 55
- Current System: Excel + small accounting software

---

## 📍 Business Problem

Gricae Enterprises experiences:

- Manual processes
- Poor visibility of inventory
- Delayed financial reporting
- No integration between purchasing and sales

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

To implement **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations** to:

- Integrate operations
- Improve financial control
- Enable reporting
- Manage inventory and procurement

---

# 📋 Business Requirements

## 💰 Finance

- Chart of Accounts

- Monthly closing process
- VAT reporting

---

## 📦 Procurement & Inventory

- Vendor management
- Purchase order approval
- Inventory tracking

---

## 🛒 Sales

- Customer invoicing
- Payment tracking
- Credit control

---

## 🏦 Cash Management

- Bank account tracking
- Payment processing
- Reconciliation
